## Words and claims
- Say **"subscribers"**, never "customers", when the audience means MOPC's own users.
- MOPC is an open-access network owned by the Municipality of Pictou County.
- Brand names: MOPC Network, FibreFirst, FirstHome Wireless. Slogan: "Built by us. For us." with full stops.
- Approved secondary tagline: "reliable, affordable, and it keeps us connected."
- Unconfirmed, so don't use without client OK: "MOPC FibreMax", "The Way Internet Should Be".
- No speeds, prices, plan tiers or install fees on screen. The install fee contradicts itself across the site, and that is still unresolved.
- No competitors, ever. Bell (Gary & Lynn) and Eastlink (Brad) are spoken in the raw audio, so never cut those in.
- Every CTA routes to checking availability at the address. Nobody promises what another address gets.
- `mopcnetwork.ca/check-availability/` has no address checker, which is a known site blocker.
- Voice is plain, warm and neighbourly. No hype, no urgency, no exclamation marks.
- In Google Ads, never bid on bare "mopc", because it also means the Municipality (council, tax, garbage).

## Captions (social copy)
- No dashes used as punctuation.
- Every caption has hashtags, a location tag, and ends with a CTA.
- Captions are burned into every Meta cut, since feeds start muted.
- Keep captions out of the top 250 px and bottom 400 px on 9:16, and never over the action, a face or the payoff.

## People on camera
- **No direct eye contact with the lens.** People talk to the interviewer just beside the camera. Trim every glance to camera, including faces on screens.
- **Face first:** every voice belongs to a face we see. No voiceover from someone never shown.
- The interviewer's or crew's voice never appears in a cut. Bloopers are the only exception, and they need client approval.
- Use only the final clean take of a line, not the rough first tries.
- Questions, not scripted lines. One real take beats five rehearsed ones.
- A face plus "Pictou County" appears within 3 s on Meta and 5 s on YouTube.
- Signed releases from everyone on camera, and a parent signs for any kids. No house numbers, street signs or plates.
- No third-party brands, sports logos or celebrities.
- Real people, documentary style, are preferred over AI. Any AI-generated spot must be disclosed.
- No fake Pictou County locations. Red sand means PEI, not Pictou.

## Video house style (from shipped V1/V2/V3 and Sep 28 reel)
- 9:16 at 1080×1920. Shoot or deliver 16:9 as well for YouTube.
- Hard cuts, about 2 to 2.5 s per shot. No picture-in-picture and no title cards.
- Grade warmer and more saturated than the raw S-Log footage.
- Captions: purple pill RGB(120,77,158) with white Montserrat Bold, or white Montserrat with shadow (V1–V3).
- Name tag: white box with navy Montserrat text, top-left.
- End card: light background RGB(241,242,245), colour logo, green pill RGB(137,187,59) reading "Check your address." for consumers. Business pieces use "Reliable when your work depends on it." with "Get a Quote". Hard cut, about 3.5 s.
- Colours: navy #284078, purple #794E9E, green #99C15C. Fonts: Josefin Sans (titles) and Montserrat (body).
- Loudness: −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP. Music ducked well under the voice.
- No ElevenLabs.
- Music must be licensed or public domain. AI-generated beds need their commercial terms checked before paid use.
- Before delivery, QC every clip: setting logic, physics, eye contact, continuity, screens, locale, stray text and logos, audio sync, and caption placement.
