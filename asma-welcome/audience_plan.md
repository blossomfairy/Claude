# Asma welcome – audience plan (Facebook + Instagram)

**Target audience:** people in north Saskatoon who could book at Chatters, Lawson Heights Mall (134 Primrose Dr, Bay 30). Existing clients, mall shoppers, and residents of the nearby neighbourhoods.

## 1. Organic setup (free, do on every post)
| Setting | Instagram | Facebook |
|---|---|---|
| Location tag | Lawson Heights Mall (or the Chatters Lawson Heights place) | Check in at Chatters – Lawson Heights |
| Collab / tag | Send a Collab invite to the Lawson Heights Mall account so the post shows to its followers | Tag the Lawson Heights Mall page |
| Tag Asma | Only if Asma has a public work account and agrees | Same |
| Caption | `caption.txt` (5 hashtags max) | `caption_facebook.txt` (address included, 1–2 hashtags) |
| Alt text | "Asma, new team member at Chatters Lawson Heights Mall, smiling in a black turtleneck." | Same |
| Reel audio | Add a trending upbeat track in-app (the file is silent) | Same |
| Share | Share the reel to Stories with a location sticker and a "Book now" link sticker | Share to the page's Story |
| Cross-post | Post the reel to Instagram and Facebook as a Reel. Post the 4:5 image to the Facebook feed. | |

Post the first comment yourself (e.g. "Come say hi to Asma this week! 👋"). Reply to every comment within the first hour.

## 2. Boost settings (optional, Meta Ads Manager / "Boost")
- **Goal:** Awareness (reach) or Engagement. Avoid "Messages" unless someone is monitoring the inbox.
- **Location:** Drop a pin on Lawson Heights Mall, **8–10 km radius**, "People living in this location". This covers north Saskatoon without the whole province.
- **Age:** 18–65+. Don't narrow by gender. Salon clients are broad, and Meta's Advantage+ audience works better with fewer limits.
- **Interests (as suggestions only):** Hair care, Beauty salons, Hairstyles, Shopping malls.
- **Custom audiences (best results):** people who engaged with the page/IG in the last 365 days, plus a lookalike of past clients if a client list is available.
- **Budget:** $5–10 CAD per day for 5–7 days is enough to test. Check results after 48 h.
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements (Feed, Reels, Stories on both apps).
- **Button:** "Book now", linked to the Chatters online booking page for Lawson Heights.

## 3. Timing
- Post between Tuesday and Thursday, either 11 am–1 pm or 6–8 pm Saskatchewan time (CST, no DST). These are general best-practice windows. The account's own Insights → "Most active times" overrides them.

## 4. Check before posting
- Chatters is a franchise brand: confirm local stores may post staff welcomes on the location's own pages, and follow any corporate brand rules.
- Get Asma's OK to post the photo and name.
