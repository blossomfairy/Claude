# Gabby before/after – audience plan (Facebook + Instagram)

**Target audience:** people in north Saskatoon who could book at Chatters, Lawson Heights Mall (134 Primrose Dr, Bay 30). Existing clients, mall shoppers, and residents of the nearby neighbourhoods. Colour clients in particular, especially anyone wanting to fix brassy or faded ends.

## 1. Organic setup (free, do on every post)
| Setting | Instagram | Facebook |
|---|---|---|
| Format | Reel `gabby_before_after_reel.mp4` (9:16) | Reel + feed image `gabby_before_after_post.jpg` (4:5) |
| Location tag | Lawson Heights Mall (or the Chatters Lawson Heights place) | Check in at Chatters – Lawson Heights |
| Collab / tag | Collab invite to the Lawson Heights Mall account | Tag the Lawson Heights Mall page |
| Tag Gabby | Only if Gabby has a public work account and agrees | Same |
| Tag the client | No. Both photos show only the back of the head; keep the post anonymous unless the client asks to be tagged | Same |
| Caption | `caption.txt` (5 hashtags max) | `caption_facebook.txt` (address included, 1–2 hashtags) |
| Alt text | "Before and after: Long hair with brassy orange ends changed to an even, rich dark brunette with soft waves, by Gabby at Chatters Lawson Heights." | Same |
| Reel audio | Add a trending upbeat track in-app (the file is silent) | Same |
| Cover | Pick the side-by-side frame (about 12s) as the Reel cover | Same |
| Story | Post `gabby_story.jpg` (9:16). Put the "Book now" link sticker and location sticker in the empty band under "Tap the link below to book" | Same, on the page's Story |

Post the first comment yourself (e.g. "Which look do you love more – before or after? 👀"). Reply to every comment within the first hour.

## 2. Boost settings (optional, Meta Ads Manager / "Boost")
- **Goal:** Awareness (reach) or Engagement. Avoid "Messages" unless someone is monitoring the inbox.
- **Location:** Drop a pin on Lawson Heights Mall, **8–10 km radius**, "People living in this location".
- **Age:** 18–65+. Don't narrow by gender; Advantage+ audience works better with fewer limits.
- **Interests (as suggestions only):** Hair colouring, Highlights, Beauty salons, Hairstyles.
- **Custom audiences (best results):** people who engaged with the page/IG in the last 365 days, plus a lookalike of past clients if a client list is available.
- **Budget:** $5–10 CAD per day for 5–7 days is enough to test. Check results after 48 h.
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements (Feed, Reels, Stories on both apps).
- **Button:** "Book now", linked to the Chatters online booking page for Lawson Heights.

## 3. Timing
- Post between Tuesday and Thursday, either 11 am–1 pm or 6–8 pm Saskatchewan time (CST, no DST). These are general best-practice windows. The account's own Insights → "Most active times" overrides them.
- Space it at least 2–3 days from the Afia before/after post so the two don't compete.

## 4. Check before posting
- Get the client's written OK to use her photos and video on social media, even though her face isn't shown.
- Get Gabby's OK to be named.
- Chatters is a franchise brand: follow any corporate brand rules for location pages.
