# MOPC Network: "Reliable Connection" video concepts

## Reference videos

| Link | What it is |
|---|---|
| vimeo.com/421439852 | Etisalat "Moonwalk", a 69s telecom spot (Finnish crew). I only had access to the metadata, not the footage. |
| Facebook reel 1484771422941015 | A comedy reel of someone on their phone walking into a pile of dirt. |

What MOPC can borrow from these: a simple visual joke with no dialogue, someone too absorbed in their screen to notice the real world, and a short runtime with the payoff at the end.

## Guardrails

- The ad claims reliability through story, not through specs. It shows no speeds, prices or "never drops" promises.
- Every version ends on the line "See what's available at your address."
- Nobody looks directly into the camera, including during any video calls shown on screen.
- Lighting is natural, the setting is rural Pictou County, and no third-party logos appear.
- End cards use navy `#284078` with a green `#99C15C` accent. Josefin Sans for supers, Montserrat for the URL.

## Concept A (recommended): "The only thing that dropped"

- **Persona:** Backyard Boomer Bob
- **Objective:** Awareness, then an availability check
- **Formats:** 30s, plus a 15s cutdown, 9:16 and 16:9

**Idea:** Bob is on a video call with his granddaughter and wanders his whole property while they talk. The call never hiccups. He doesn't watch where he's going and topples gently into a pile of garden soil. The call is fine.

| # | Time | Shot | Audio / Super |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–3s | Kitchen. Bob, 65, laughing at his tablet, looking down at the screen. | SFX: kids giggling through the tablet |
| 2 | 3–8s | He walks out the back door still talking. Over the shoulder we see the granddaughter on screen. | Granddaughter (VO): "Grampy, show me the garden!" |
| 3 | 8–14s | Tracking shot across the yard. He steps around a wheelbarrow and ducks a clothesline without looking up. | Light acoustic music builds |
| 4 | 14–18s | Wide shot. Bob steps backwards toward a fresh mound of soil. | Music beat drops out |
| 5 | 18–21s | He sits down into the soil in a soft, comedic fall. The tablet stays held up and the picture stays crisp. | SFX: soft thump. Granddaughter laughs. |
| 6 | 21–25s | Close-up. Bob, covered in dirt, grins at the screen. | Super: **The only thing that dropped was Bob.** |
| 7 | 25–30s | End card | Super: **A connection you can count on.** / VO: "Fibre or wireless, from your local team. See what's available at your address." / mopcnetwork.ca |

**15s cutdown:** shots 3, 5, 6 and 7.

**Production notes:** Shoot this on a real Pictou County property in natural light. Use a soft, staged soil pile as a safe landing. The on-screen call content is composited in post, and the granddaughter looks at her screen, not the lens.

## Concept C: "Local team" (lowest cost)

- **Persona:** Old Town Road Olivia
- **Objective:** Trust
- **Format:** 15s, 9:16

**Idea:** Olivia trips over a garden hose while scrolling on her phone, a direct nod to the reel. Her neighbour helps her up and says, "Should've looked up. At least your internet's steady." The super reads **Reliable internet, backed by people right here in Pictou County.** It ends on the availability check line.
